HOW THE LOFT WORKS

Six columns, five rows of nest boxes. Land 8 or more of the same symbol anywhere to win. Winning symbols clear out and new ones drop in, so one spin can pay again and again.

HOMING BIRDS

A pigeon that lands in a box flies off. The number on its yellow band counts the spins until it comes home. When it lands it brings a capsule: a coin worth 0.2× to 100× the bet it left on. Some bring a whistle that calls every bird in the air home at once. Up to 6 birds can be out.

RACE DAY

3 race clocks start 10 free spins, 4 give 12, 5 or more give 15. Every bird in the air comes home first. On Race Day each released bird is back on the next spin with a multiplier capsule: ×2 up to ×100. Multipliers add up in the loft and stay for the whole race, multiplying every win. 3 clocks during the race add 5 spins.
